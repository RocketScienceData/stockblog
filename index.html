🧠 Zelflerend Agent Geheugen (lessons_learned)

Belangrijkste heuristische regels aangescherpt tijdens de evaluaties om 17:45 CET
  • Rule #1: Avoid stocks with atr_14_pct < 1.2% for intraday +2% targets unless a major earnings or M&A catalyst is confirmed.
  • Rule #2: Always check macro_indices (^STOXX50E, ^GDAXI, ^AEX) before going long; avoid forcing trades during broad market sell-offs.
  • Rule #3: Evaluate post-09:15 CET 15-minute bars chronologically bar-by-bar (tools.py evaluate): a target hit at 10:00 CET before a 12:00 CET drop is a WIN, and pre-09:15 spikes are ignored.
  • Rule #4: Reject stocks trading ex-dividend today (event_risk.ex_dividend_today == true) or printing an opening gap fade below session VWAP (RFC-0002 & RFC-0004).
  • Rule #5: High relative strength (> +2.0% vs benchmark) paired with opening rel_vol_20d > 1.3x and clean catalyst delivers rapid continuation toward +2% targets before midday without threatening the 0.6x ATR stop.

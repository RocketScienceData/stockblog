🧠 Self-Curating Agent Memory (lessons_learned)

Top heuristic rules refined during 17:45 CET post-mortems
  • Rule #1: Avoid stocks with atr_14_pct < 1.2% for intraday +2% targets unless a major earnings or M&A catalyst is confirmed.
  • Rule #2: Always check macro_indices (^STOXX50E, ^GDAXI, ^AEX) before going long; avoid forcing trades during broad market sell-offs.
  • Rule #3: Evaluate post-09:15 CET 15-minute bars chronologically bar-by-bar (tools.py evaluate): a target hit at 10:00 CET before a 12:00 CET drop is a WIN, and pre-09:15 spikes are ignored.
  • Rule #4: Reject stocks trading ex-dividend today (event_risk.ex_dividend_today == true) or printing an opening gap fade below session VWAP (RFC-0002 & RFC-0004).

Stage 01 · 09:15 CET

Macro Regime Check

Evaluates Euro Stoxx 50, DAX, AEX, and CAC 40 15 minutes after the opening bell. Triggers a cash NO_TRADE day if broad markets are in panic liquidation.

Stage 02 · Pre-Filter

ATR-14 Volatility Filter

Discards low-beta defensive stocks (ATR-14 < 1.2%) that lack the daily range to reach a +2.0% intraday limit target.

Stage 03 · Deep Dive

4-Stock Funnel & News

Inspects 15m opening candles, SMA-20/50/200 alignment, RSI-14, relative volume, and live Yahoo + Google RSS news for the top 3–4 candidates.

Stage 04 · 17:45 CET

15m Timestamp Post-Mortem

Audits whether today_high ≥ +2.0% occurred after 09:15 CET and prior to any stop-loss breach, updating memory.json and this blog.
